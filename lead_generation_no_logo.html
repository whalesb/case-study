Web Scraping & Qualification Flow

Complete Automation Flow
Phase-by-Phase Implementation
1
Scheduled Trigger & Scraping
  • Scheduled trigger initiates recurring workflow (daily/weekly)
  • Apify Google Maps Scraper activates with keywords & locations
  • Extracts business listings (e.g., "plumber" + "New York")
  • Raw data output prepared for filtering
2
Intelligent Lead Qualification
  • Filtering module applies qualification criteria
  • Validates if business has a website (Has Website? decision)
  • Businesses without websites → Discarded
  • Qualified businesses → Added to processing queue
3
Contact Detail Extraction
  • Iterate through each qualified business
  • Deploy Apify Contact Detail Scraper on websites
  • Extract and verify contact information (email, phone)
  • Structured data aggregation with business details
4
Data Aggregation & Storage
  • Consolidate business details, URLs, and contacts
  • Google Sheets integration for automated data entry
  • Save as new rows in actionable lead database
  • Sales & marketing teams receive ready-to-use leads
