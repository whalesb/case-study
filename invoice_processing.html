Problem Statement
Finance department receives hundreds of invoices each month from multiple vendors and internal cost centers. The existing invoice processing was highly manual — employees spent hours extracting data from invoices, verifying vendor details, obtaining approvals, and updating the ERP system.
Manual Data Entry

Email-Driven Approvals

Lack of Integration
Disconnected systems required duplicate data entry across platforms
High Error Rate

Phase-by-Phase Implementation
1
Invoice Capture & Digitization
  • Automated email monitoring for invoice attachments
  • Cloud folder synchronization for bulk uploads
  • OCR-based data extraction using Gemini AI
  • Structured data storage in Airtable
2
Data Extraction & Validation
  • Extract header, line items, and vendor details
  • Apply business rules and validation checks
  • PO matching against existing purchase orders
  • Duplicate detection and exception flagging
3
Approval Workflow Automation
  • Route to appropriate department based on category
  • Multi-level approval (Finance, Operations, Procurement)
  • Exception handling for no-match scenarios
  • Real-time status tracking and notifications
4
ERP Integration & Reporting
  • Automatic posting to QuickBooks upon approval
  • GL account updates and payment entry recording
  • Real-time dashboards and exception monitoring
  • Complete audit trail and compliance logs
