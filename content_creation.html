Multi-Agent Workflow

AI Content Creation Pipeline

Complete Automation Flow
Agent-by-Agent Breakdown
1
Signal Agent (Data Intelligence)
  • Scrapes industry news, trends, and data sources
  • Monitors competitor content and market insights
  • Aggregates relevant data for content opportunities
  • Passes structured data to Topic Agent
2
Topic Agent (Content Strategy)
  • Clusters scraped data into thematic groups
  • Identifies high-value content angles and opportunities
  • Pre-filters ideas based on relevance criteria
  • Routes approved themes to validation agents
3
Brand Guardian & Scoring Agents
  • Brand Guardian filters against client guidelines
  • Ensures tone, style, and messaging alignment
  • Scoring Agent evaluates engagement potential
  • Only approved ideas proceed to content creation
4
Writer & Editor Agents (Production)
  • Writer Agent drafts LinkedIn posts, blogs, and copy
  • Content optimized for engagement and SEO
  • Editor Agent (Master) performs QA review
  • Delivers polished, ready-to-publish content
