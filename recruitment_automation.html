Problem Statement
Tech Bake Ltd's recruitment team managed candidate outreach and job applications entirely through manual processes in Bullhorn CRM. Consultants spent hours each day calling candidates, updating records, and coordinating placements. The existing recruitment workflow was highly manual — staff spent excessive time on repetitive tasks, limiting the number of job openings they could actively manage.
