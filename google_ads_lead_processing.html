Lead Processing Pipeline

Gmail to CRM Automation

Complete Automation Flow
Phase-by-Phase Implementation
1
Lead Capture & AI Processing
  • Shopify orders automatically synced to OpenAI ChatGPT
  • Gmail inbox receives lead email and triggers workflow
  • OpenAI ChatGPT Analysis monitors database 24/7
  • AI processes and extracts structured lead data
2
Data Extraction & Analysis
  • Extract contact details (name, email, phone)
  • Identify inquiry type (product interest, pricing, demo)
  • Calculate lead score based on engagement indicators
  • All data structured for CRM integration
3
CRM Profile Creation
  • Make.com aggregates extracted structured data
  • Automatically creates detailed contact profile
  • Profile includes contact info, inquiry type, lead score
  • All communications sent via GoHighLevel CRM
4
Lead Qualification & Output
  • OpenAI ChatGPT updated with renewal status in real-time
  • Qualified Lead Ready for Follow-up output generated
  • Sales team receives real-time notification
  • Zero manual data entry required - 100% automated
