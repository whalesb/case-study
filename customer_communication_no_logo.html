AI Communication Pipeline

Automated Inquiry Processing

Complete Automation Flow
Phase-by-Phase Implementation
1
Form Capture & AI Analysis
  • Customer inquiry submitted via website form
  • n8n automation workflow triggered instantly
  • Form data extracted and sent to OpenAI GPT-4
  • AI analyzes context, categorizes intent (New/Support/General)
2
Parallel Processing & Response
  • AI generates professional, context-aware email response
  • Gmail integration sends auto-response to customer
  • Parallel: Google Sheets logs structured inquiry data
  • New row created with name, email, message, category
3
Documentation & Archival
  • Convergence point aggregates all key details
  • Google Docs Generator creates formal intake record
  • Structured client documentation with full context
  • Document saved to shared Google Drive folder
4
Notification & Completion
  • Permanent record saved in Google Drive
  • Team alert notification sent to staff
  • Complete audit trail of inquiry and response
  • Ready for follow-up by appropriate team member
