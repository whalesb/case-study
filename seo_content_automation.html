SEO Content Pipeline

End-to-End Publishing Automation

Complete Automation Flow
Phase-by-Phase Implementation
1
Keyword Research & Topic Selection
  • Make.com scheduled trigger initiates workflow
  • SEMrush API integration conducts keyword research
  • Analyzes high-opportunity topics (search volume, difficulty)
  • Keywords Found? Yes → Data Transfer; No → Alert Notification
2
AI Content Generation
  • Keywords and topics passed to ChatGPT-4 agent
  • Generates SEO-optimized article with meta descriptions
  • Includes structured data and proper formatting
  • Draft complete with all SEO elements
3
SEO Optimization & Quality Check
  • Make.com workflow applies on-page optimization
  • SEO rules: headers, internal links, keyword density
  • Final content quality check performed
  • Optimized content ready for publishing
4
Auto-Publishing & Analytics
  • CMS integration (WordPress API) auto-publishes content
  • Content published live on client website
  • Parallel logging: Google Sheets tracks performance
  • Notification service sends team alert and confirmation
