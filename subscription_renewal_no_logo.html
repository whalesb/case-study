The client's customer management team was overwhelmed by the tedious, error-prone process of managing thousands of software subscription renewals. Manual Data Entry

Fragmented Communications

Lack of Integration

High Error Rate

Subscription Renewal Pipeline

Automated Customer Lifecycle

Complete Automation Flow
Phase-by-Phase Implementation
1
Data Integration & Monitoring
  • Shopify orders automatically synced to Google Sheets
  • Make.com Data Sync Engine stores and tracks subscriptions
  • AI Subscription Assistant monitors database 24/7
  • Expiry monitoring at 30/14/3 day intervals
2
Intelligent Customer Segmentation
  • Product Status Check verifies discontinued items
  • Customer Segmentation identifies high-volume users (10+ purchases)
  • Email Preferences Check validates do-not-email list
  • Substitute Products List referenced for discontinued items
3
Multi-Stage Renewal Campaigns
  • High Volume Detection (10+ purchases) → Auto-Renewal
  • Regular customers → Do-Not-Email Filter applied
  • Allowed customers receive 30-Day, 14-Day, 3-Day Reminders
  • All communications sent via Gmail API
4
Status Tracking & Optimization
  • Google Sheets updated with renewal status in real-time
  • Discontinued Item Check triggers substitute product emails
  • Complete audit trail maintained for all interactions
  • Performance metrics tracked for continuous optimization
